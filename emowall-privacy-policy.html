Privacy Policy

Emowall AI 2.0 — Your Family's Guardian

Effective date: October 2026

About this app

Emowall is a personal safety app. It is meant to be used by or under the supervision of a parent, guardian or school. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and how you can delete it.

Data we use and why

• Account — your sign-in details (email, name, user ID) through Firebase Authentication.

• Emergency contacts — name, relation and phone number of the people you add. Stored on our server (Supabase) so alerts can be delivered.

• Location — used to detect unsafe zones and to share live location with your emergency contacts during an SOS.

• Voice — while Active Protection is on, the app listens for danger words using the Android speech recognition service, which is provided by Google and may process audio on Google's servers. Emowall does not record audio and does not store it.

• Photos and videos you choose — in Animal Alert, the photo is sent through our server function (Cloudflare Workers) to Anthropic's AI service to identify the animal. In Media Check, the photo or video you choose is sent to Google's Gemini AI. These are sent only when you start the scan. Emowall does not keep a copy.

• Profile photo — if you choose to save one, it is stored on our server (Supabase).

• Health Connect (heart rate, blood oxygen) — read only after you agree in the app, to detect possible physical distress as one signal during Kidnap Mode. This is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything. A reading may be included in an alert message if it triggers one.

• Motion sensor — used on the phone for fall and shake detection.

• Alert details — when an SOS is triggered, the alert contains the name, the reason, and the location.

Who receives data (service providers)

We use these providers only to run the features above:

• Google (Firebase sign-in, Android speech recognition, Gemini)

• Supabase (emergency contacts, profile photo)

• Twilio (sending SMS alerts to your emergency contacts)

• Telegram and Discord (alert channels operated by the Emowall team)

• Cloudflare and Anthropic (Animal Alert image analysis)

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

• Emergency contacts and your profile photo are kept until you delete them in the app or ask us to delete your account.

• Photos sent for Animal Alert and Media Check are not saved by Emowall.

• To delete your account and all related data, email meradivin@gmail.com. We will process the request within 30 days.

Permissions

Location, microphone, camera, Health Connect (heart rate and blood oxygen), notifications, and internet. Each permission is requested in the app with an explanation, and you can withdraw it at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

Emowall is intended to be set up and controlled by a parent, guardian or school. Emergency contacts and alerts are managed by the adult account holder.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Access to emergency contacts and profile photos goes through server functions that verify your sign-in before returning or changing anything.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
