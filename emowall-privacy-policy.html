Privacy Policy

Emowall AI 2.0 - Your Family's Guardian
Developer: Dwin Universe
Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information Emowall AI 2.0 ("Emowall", "the app", "we") collects, why we collect it, and who receives it. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Emergency contacts. Names, relationships and phone numbers that you add. They are stored on our server (Supabase) so that alerts can be sent to them.
  • Location. Collected while the app is in use. When an SOS or safety alert is active, your location is sent to our server and shared with your emergency contacts, for example through a live tracking link. This release does not collect location while the app is closed.
  • Voice and sound. The microphone is used for voice commands and sound detection. Speech recognition may be handled by the speech service of your device or Google. We do not keep audio recordings on our servers.
  • Camera, photos and media. If you use features that capture or analyze photos, video or audio (such as evidence recording or Media Verifier), that content is used to provide the feature and may be sent to AI service providers for analysis. You can also upload a profile photo.
  • Health and sensor data. Data from your connected smartwatch or Health Connect (such as heart rate and motion) is used to detect emergencies. It may be sent to our server and stored so that alerts can be raised and reviewed.
  • AI features. Text or voice you give to AI features (for example Emo Care, Child Doctor AI, Digital Amma, Women's Health) is sent to third-party AI service providers to generate a reply.
  • Animal incident records. If you report an attack or bite, we record the type of animal or creature you select, the time and the place, so that first-aid information and a summary for a doctor can be shown.
  • Account data. Login details (such as email address or phone number) handled through Firebase.

2. How we use the information

  • To detect emergencies and send alerts (push notifications and SMS) to your emergency contacts.
  • To show safety and first-aid information.
  • To improve the quality of safety analysis with AI services.
  • To provide, secure and support the app and your subscription.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who receives information

  • Your emergency contacts, only when an alert is sent.
  • Service providers that help us run the app: Supabase (database and server functions), Google (Firebase, Google Play, Health Connect), Twilio (SMS delivery) and third-party AI service providers. They receive only the information needed to provide their service.
  • Authorities or others if required by law or to protect someone's life.

Payments for subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your card details.

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, where our providers operate.

4. Children

Emowall is intended for adults. Parents or guardians may use it to protect a child, and any account or contact details for a child must be created and managed by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us information without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Not a medical device

Emowall provides general safety and first-aid information. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it cannot guarantee that an emergency will be detected or that an alert will be delivered. In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.

6. Permissions

The app may ask for: location, microphone, camera, notifications, and access to health and sensor data from your watch. You can turn permissions off in your phone settings, but some safety features will then stop working.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your emergency contacts inside the app. To delete your account and all data linked to it, email info@emothewall.online from your registered account and we will delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep some records.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to some uses. Contact us at the email below to use these rights.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with reputable providers. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when features or data practices change and will change the date above.
