Privacy Policy

Emowall AI 2.0 — Your Family's Guardian

Effective Date: September 2026

Introduction

Emowall AI ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and safety of the families, children, and schools who use our app. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and how you can control it.

Data We Collect

• Location data — Used for Guardian Mode GPS tracking and safe-zone alerts, so that a parent or guardian can be notified if a child leaves a designated safe area.

• Microphone / Audio data — Used to detect distress sounds, crying, or threatening speech. Audio is processed on-device in real time and is not recorded or stored on our servers.

• Camera — Used only during an active Shield Mode emergency to capture evidence. The camera is not used for continuous monitoring or surveillance.

• Health Connect data (heart rate, blood oxygen) and body sensor data — Used, with your explicit permission, solely to detect signs of physical distress (such as an abnormal heart rate or a fall) and to trigger a safety alert. This is not used for any medical diagnosis or health advice.

• Emergency contacts — Phone numbers you provide for a parent, relative, or school principal, used only to deliver SOS alerts.

• Account information — Basic authentication data (e.g. email) used to identify your account and link it to your configured emergency contacts and settings.

How We Use Your Data

• Location, audio, and sensor data are used exclusively to power real-time safety detection and alerting features.

• Health and sensor data never leaves your device except as part of an emergency alert that you or the app's detection system has triggered.

• Emergency contact information is used only to deliver the alert messages you have configured the app to send.

• We do not use any of this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to child and family safety.

How Emergency Alerts Are Delivered (Third-Party Processors)

When an emergency is detected or manually triggered, Emowall sends an alert containing the child's name, the reason for the alert, and location to the emergency contacts you have configured. To deliver this reliably, we use the following third-party processors, each acting only under our instructions:

• Supabase — securely stores emergency contact information and coordinates alert delivery.

• Twilio — delivers SMS alert messages to your configured emergency contacts.

• Telegram and Discord — optional additional alert channels, governed by their own respective privacy policies when used.

• Firebase (Google) — used for account authentication and core app infrastructure, subject to Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention & Deletion

• Location, audio, and sensor data are processed in real time for detection purposes and are not stored on our servers.

• Emergency contact numbers are retained only for as long as your account remains active.

• Health tracking and child mood data are stored locally on your device only, and are never uploaded to our servers.

• You may request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing meradivin@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 30 days.

Permissions We Request

• Location — Guardian Mode GPS and safe-zone alerts

• Microphone — Sound and distress detection

• Camera — Evidence capture during an active Shield Mode emergency

• Health Connect / Body Sensors — Heart rate and blood oxygen monitoring for physical distress detection

• Phone / Contacts — Emergency SOS calling

• Notifications — Safety alerts and guardian notifications

Each permission is used strictly for the safety feature described above, and you will be shown an in-app explanation before any permission is requested.

Children's Privacy

Emowall is built to protect children, and all child safety features are configured and controlled by a parent, guardian, or authorized school administrator — never by the child directly. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what is strictly necessary to provide these safety features, and wherever possible, child-related data (such as mood tracking) is processed and stored locally on the device.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. Emergency contact data is protected using row-level security on our backend database and is accessible only through authenticated requests.

Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your data, and withdraw any permission at any time through your device settings or by contacting us directly at the email below.

Changes to This Policy
